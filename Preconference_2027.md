The fourth computational preconference will be held at the 2027 <a href="https://socialaffectiveneuro.org/preconference-workshop/">SANS</a> annual meeting in Montreal, Canada.

This preconference will provide a dedicated space to share, discuss, and build community around theory-driven cognitive computational modeling in social and affective neuroscience. We aim to highlight how computational modeling can link formal theories to complex data, and will illustrate different modeling techniques (e.g., feature encoding models, multi-agent reinforcement learning, information theory) to understand social and affective phenomena. Finally, we will discuss and contextualize related future challenges and opportunities for the field. This preconference is open to people with and without prior experience in computational modeling.
